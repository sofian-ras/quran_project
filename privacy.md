# Privacy Policy – Quran App

**Last updated: January 18, 2026**

We respect your privacy. This app is intended solely to provide access to the Quran.

## 1. Data collected

- **Local storage**: to save reading progress and user preferences.  
- **Audio**: used only for Quran playback.  
- **Analytics** (optional): if included, to improve the app (anonymized, no personal data).

## 2. How data is used

Collected data is used only to:  

- Save reading progress and preferences. 
- Ensure the proper functioning of the app.  

No data is shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

## 3. Deletion or information requests

You can delete your local data by uninstalling the app.  
For any questions about your data, contact us at: `najsapro@gmail.com`.
